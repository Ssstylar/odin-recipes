Pizza

Description

A crisp-edged, chewy-centered pizza topped with tangy tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and slices of spicy pepperoni that curl and crisp in the oven. It's simple, familiar, and hard to get wrong.

Origin: Pizza comes from Naples, Italy. Pepperoni pizza itself is an Italian-American invention, developed in the US. In Italy, "peperoni" means bell peppers, so ordering one there gets you a completely different pizza.

Quick facts:

  • Prep time: 20mins(plus 1 to 2 hours for the dough to rise)
  • Cook time: 12-15 minutes at 245 degree celcuis
  • Total time: about 2 hours
  • Serves: 4

Ingredients

Dough

  • 500g (4 cups) all purpose flour
  • 7g (2 tsp) instant yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Sauce

  • 400g crushed tomatotoes
  • 2 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Toppings

  • 400g low moisture mozzarella, shreded
  • 80 to 100g pepperoni
  • Grated Parmesan, chilli flakes, fresh basil
