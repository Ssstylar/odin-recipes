Pizza

Description

A crisp-edged, chewy-centered pizza topped with tangy tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and slices of spicy pepperoni that curl and crisp in the oven. It's simple, familiar, and hard to get wrong.

Origin: Pizza comes from Naples, Italy. Pepperoni pizza itself is an Italian-American invention, developed in the US. In Italy, "peperoni" means bell peppers, so ordering one there gets you a completely different pizza.

Quick facts:

  • Prep time: 20mins(plus 1 to 2 hours for the dough to rise)
  • Cook time: 12-15 minutes at 245 degree celcuis
  • Total time: about 2 hours
  • Serves: 4

Ingredients

Dough

  • 500g (4 cups) all purpose flour
  • 7g (2 tsp) instant yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 300ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Sauce

  • 400g crushed tomatotoes
  • 2 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Toppings

  • 400g low moisture mozzarella, shreded
  • 80 to 100g pepperoni
  • Grated Parmesan, chilli flakes, fresh basil

Steps

Dough (20 min prep + 2 hours rising)

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a large bowl.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil. Stir until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Knead on a lightly floured surface for 8 to 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm spot for 1 to 2 hours, until doubled.

Sauce (5 mins)

  1. Mix the crushed tomatoes, garlic, oregano, salt, and olive oil. Donot cook it. It cooks on the pizza.

Asemble and Bake (12 to 15 mins)

  1. Preheat the oven to 245°C (475°F) with a baking tray or stone inside for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Divide the dough in two. Stretch each piece by hand to about 30 cm (12 in). Avoid a rolling pin, which pushes out the air bubbles.
  3. Dust a sheet of baking paper with cornmeal and place the dough on it.
  4. Spread the sauce, leaving a 2 cm border for the crust.
  5. Top with mozzarella, then pepperoni.
  6. Slide onto the hot tray and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Rest for 2 minutes, then slice and serve.
