Jollof Rice (Ghana Style)

Description

Long-grain rice cooked in a rich, spiced tomato and pepper base until every grain is orange-red, fluffy, and lightly smoky from the bottom of the pot. It is a centerpiece dish at Ghanaian parties, weddings, and Sunday lunches, usually served with fried plantain, grilled chicken, and shito.

Origin: Jollof traces back to the Wolof people of Senegambia in West Africa. Today, Ghana, Nigeria, and Senegal each claim their own version, and the rivalry is half the fun. Ghana-style versions usually use fragrant long-grain rice, ginger, and a little curry powder.

Quick facts:

  • Prep time: 20 minutes
  • Cook time: 50-60 minutes
  • Total time: about 1 hour 20 minutes
  • Serves: 4-6

Ingredients

Rice

  • 600 g (3 cups) long-grain parboiled rice or basmati

Tomato Base

  • 4 medium ripe tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 to 2 scotch bonnets, to taste
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves

For Cooking

  • 80 ml (⅓ cup) vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 stock cubes
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 480 ml (2 cups) chicken or vegetable stock

To Serve

  • Fried plantain, grilled chicken, shito, or sliced tomato and onion (optional)

Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, bell pepper, scotch bonnet, large onion, ginger, and garlic until smooth.
  2. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well.
  3. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Fry the sliced onion for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato paste and fry for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until it darkens.
  5. Pour in the blended mix. Cook uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, until thick and the oil rises to the surface.
  6. Add the curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, stock cubes, and salt. Stir.
  7. Add the stock and bring to a boil.
  8. Stir in the rice once, making sure all grains are submerged.
  9. Cover the pot with foil, then the lid, to seal in steam.
  10. Cook on the lowest heat for 30 to 35 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  11. Fluff gently with a fork, then rest for 5 minutes.
  12. Serve hot with your sides.
